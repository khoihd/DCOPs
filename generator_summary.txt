Generator Summary
=================

Instance generators in pydcop/commands/generators/:

- agents: generates agent definitions, capacities, hosting costs, and route
  costs.
- graph_coloring: generates hard or soft graph coloring DCOPs on random,
  scale-free, or grid graphs.
- iot: generates an IoT-style binary DCOP with a power-law graph, agents, and
  an initial factor-graph distribution.
- ising: generates Ising grid DCOPs with unary and binary costs, optionally
  with distribution files.
- meetings: generates PEAV meeting scheduling DCOPs, optionally with agents
  and a distribution.
- scenario: generates scenario YAML files for Dynamic DCOPs. It currently
  creates timed agent-removal events, not static DCOP instances.
- secp: generates SECP-style DCOPs with lights, model variables, rules, and
  one agent per light.
- small_world: generates a Barabasi-Albert small-world-like DCOP with random
  binary costs and fixed heterogeneous agents.

Notes:
- Each generator should keep its own CLI arguments as they are now.
- small_world is not complete and is for experimental use only.
