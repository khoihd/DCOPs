DPOP join() optimization actionable steps
========================================

Goal
----
Optimize pydcop/dcop/relations.py::join() for DPOP without changing its public
behavior.


Current problem
---------------
join() currently enumerates every assignment in the joined relation and writes
each value with NAryMatrixRelation.set_value_for_assignment().

set_value_for_assignment() returns a new relation and copies the full matrix on
every write, so join() does repeated full-matrix copies inside its innermost
loop.
